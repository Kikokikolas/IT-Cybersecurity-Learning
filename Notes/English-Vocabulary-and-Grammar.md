### Grammar and Usage

| Before | Correct | Explicação |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The communication between the phone and the antenna is made in radio frequences(RF) | Communication between the phone and the antenna uses radio frequencies (RF). | Usa-se *uses radio frequencies* para explicar o meio de comunicação. |
| The phone identifies himself to the network by his SIM/eSIM | The phone identifies itself to the network through its SIM/eSIM | Para um objeto, usam-se *itself* e *its*, não *himself* e *his*. |
| Imagine that you go on a car | Imagine that you are travelling in a car | Viaja-se *in a car*; *are travelling* descreve a ação em curso. |
| the phone lets a cell | the phone leaves one cell | *Leave* significa sair; *let* significa permitir. |
| without interrupt the connection to the internet | without interrupting the internet connection | Depois de *without*, o verbo fica em *-ing*. |
| Here its different | Here it's different | *It's* significa *it is*; *its* indica posse. |
| a on ground antenna | an antenna on the ground | Usa-se *an* antes do som vocálico de *antenna*; *on the ground* indica a localização. |
| Not every satellites are at the same height | Not all satellites orbit at the same altitude. | *Every* exige singular; *all* pode acompanhar o plural *satellites*. |
| each satelite covers little areas | Each satellite covers a smaller area. | Neste contexto, cada satélite cobre uma área menor; *little* não exprime a comparação pretendida. |
| you need alot of satelites in constelation | you need a lot of satellites in a constellation | Além da ortografia, falta o artigo *a* antes de *constellation*. |
| GPS satellites orbit around 20 200km of altitude | GPS satellites orbit at an altitude of around 20,200 km | A construção é *at an altitude of*; em inglês, pode usar-se vírgula para separar milhares. |
| synced with earth rotation | in sync with Earth's rotation | O possessivo *Earth's* indica que a rotação é da Terra. |
| In our point of view its always in the same place. | From our point of view, it is always in the same place. | A expressão é *from our point of view*; aqui, *its* deveria ser *it's* ou *it is*. |
| This is used to telivision by satellite | This is used for satellite television | Usa-se *used for* antes de um nome; *used to* pode anteceder um verbo. |
| The problem about this satellites its the latency. | One problem with these satellites is latency. | Usa-se *problem with*, *these* no plural e *is* como verbo. |
| The pc creates the network packets and send them by wi fi to the router | The PC creates the network packets and sends them to the router over Wi-Fi. | Com *the PC*, os dois verbos levam «s»: *creates* e *sends*. A transmissão faz-se *over Wi-Fi*. |
| This is not a normal antenna its a phased-array antenna. | This is not a normal antenna; it's a phased-array antenna. | As duas orações precisam de separação; *it's* significa *it is*. |
| send the packet to a infrastructure | send the packet to infrastructure | Neste sentido, *infrastructure* é incontável e não leva *a*. |
| send directly to a on ground station | send it directly to a ground station | Falta o objeto *it*; *ground station* é a expressão adequada. |
| capable of redirect traffic beetwen them | capable of redirecting traffic between them | Depois de *capable of*, o verbo fica em *-ing*. |
| the satellite doesn t need | the satellite doesn't need | Falta o apóstrofo na contração de *does not*. |
| This stations are connected to earth infrastructures | These stations are connected to terrestrial infrastructure | Usa-se *these* com o plural *stations*; *terrestrial infrastructure* designa a infraestrutura em terra. |
